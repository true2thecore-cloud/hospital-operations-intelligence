# Morning Plan Ahead | 9 AM example

Date: __________  Facilitator role: __________  Source meeting ID: __________

Each report-out follows: **current state → concern → effect on today's plan → action → owner → next check**. State “not known” when information is unavailable. Follow the configured role order.

| Report-out | Questions to cover |
| --- | --- |
| Cardiac | Callouts? Start delays and causes? Pending cases? Constraints requiring support? |
| Preop | Callouts? Readiness prerequisites complete? Transport or receiving bottlenecks? Add-ons and afternoon demand? |
| PACU | Callouts? Current flow and holds? ICU or inpatient receiving capacity? Discharge and transport dependencies? |
| OR Charge | Break/lunch coverage? Room and equipment concerns? Turnover constraints? Rooms expected to remain open at 3 PM and 5 PM? |
| SPD | Callouts? Tray turnover pressure? Vendor tray needs? Afternoon readiness plan? |
| Specialty Coordinators | Special-case requirements? Sequencing dependencies? Readiness or infection-prevention concerns requiring follow-up? |
| Biomed | Equipment unavailable? Backup available? Service ticket and expected restoration? |
| Throughput | Support staff coverage? Bed/transport constraints? Owner for unresolved flow barriers? |
| Patient Experience | Readiness or communication gaps? Required follow-up before next-day arrivals? |
| Supply Chain | Backorders? Low stock or implant constraints? Delivery dates and mitigations? |
| ANM | Equipment and specialty-room sequencing? Next-day staffing coverage? Unresolved decisions? |

## Close the meeting

- Name the three constraints most likely to change the day's plan.
- Assign each an owner and a follow-up time.
- Identify exactly what the 2 PM meeting must confirm.
- Record local staffing/coverage counts only when explicitly provided. Apply local policy to clinical and staffing decisions.
