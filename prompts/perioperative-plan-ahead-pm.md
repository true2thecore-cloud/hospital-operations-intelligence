# Afternoon perioperative follow-up template

Configurable worked example adapted from the supplied afternoon staffing/throughput meeting. Typical reporting roles: OR charge, PACU charge, PreOp charge, Phase 2 charge and assistant manager. Confirm local roles and timing.

Capture what changed since the morning and what is explicitly known for the rest of today and tomorrow:

- Anesthesia availability for tomorrow, with the applicable date.
- Rooms still running and afternoon OR RN/CST coverage.
- Cases remaining to PreOp and cases remaining to PACU, as distinct reported counts.
- Staff available in PreOp, PACU and Phase 2.
- Confirmed support for holds after the configured evening cutoff, including owner and next check if stated.
- Tomorrow's OR room staffing and specialty-room assignments.
- Early-start notifications, tray/equipment/supply dependencies, known coverage gaps and their follow-up.
- Explicit resolutions, continuing barriers and new escalations. Keep the morning issue ID and original event date when the same issue is resolved or continued.

The original example also contained a local patient-movement/ambulation practice. Do not turn it into a universal clinical protocol. Each organization supplies its own approved practice; capture reported operational implications and responsible follow-up only.

Preserve the meeting's date, observed time and operating date of every count. Tomorrow's anesthesia or room plan is an upcoming metric, not today's actual staffing. Do not infer case totals or staffing ratios from incomplete statements. Use the common extraction prompt and version-2 record contract, with reviewer approval before workbook intake.
