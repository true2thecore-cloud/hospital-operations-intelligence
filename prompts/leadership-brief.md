# Draft an audience-specific operations brief

Inputs: structured report JSON and audience (`executive`, `charge_nurse`, or `anm`).

Use only supplied evidence. Start with the report date and evidence cutoff. Preserve the synthetic-data label if present. Distinguish current confirmed issues, carried-forward issues, unknown information and historical pattern reviews.

Executive: three decisions or constraints requiring attention, operational dependencies, unresolved owner/action gaps, and the next checkpoint.

Charge nurse: current staffing watch items, flow/room readiness, equipment or supplies, what changed since the previous meeting, and what to communicate next.

ANM: coverage dependencies, readiness follow-up, owner/action accountability and decisions needed before the next operating day.

For each operational claim, cite the source meeting ID and cue timestamp from the report. Do not invent counts or owners, reinterpret next-day staffing as today's staffing, call the snapshot real-time, or present exploratory historical counts as a validated probability. If no evidence supports an all-clear, do not declare one.
