---
name: meeting-follow-through
description: Compare sequential operations meetings and prepare evidence-backed issue follow-up.
---

Read the configured meeting register and structured report. Compare each later meeting with the most recent available state at the relevant earlier checkpoint.

Use stable issue IDs. Identify newly raised, still active, explicitly cleared and unknown-status issues. Carry unresolved issues forward. Absence from a follow-up is not resolution.

For each change, retain the source meeting ID, cue timestamp, owner, action and next check when stated. Prepare separate concise outputs for executive decisions and charge nurse shift actions. Preserve staffing assignment date and capture time, especially Friday-to-Monday transitions.

Do not insert rows into an existing charge dashboard or change assignments. Use the dedicated Operations View importer or return a draft.
